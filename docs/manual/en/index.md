# AlSlime User Manual

This manual explains everything from installing AlSlime to using each of its features.

Japanese version: [日本語版はこちら](../index.md)

## New to AlSlime? (Reading Order)

1. [01 Installation and Startup](01-setup.md) — From getting the app and starting it to agreeing to the Terms of Use
2. [02 Understanding the Screen](02-screen.md) — The screen layout and the buttons in the header of the conversation screen
3. [03 Your First Conversation](03-first-chat.md) — From starting a conversation to the operations on responses

To adjust the conversation settings in detail, continue with [04 Setup: Characters](04-prepare-characters.md), [05 Setup: Scene, Style, and Presets](05-prepare-scene.md), [06 Continuing a Conversation](06-continue.md), and [07 Materials](07-material.md), in that order.

How to use the supporter features is explained in [09 Supporter Features](09-sponsor.md). Image generation is explained in [10 Image Generation](10-image-generation.md) and in [07-3 Materials: Characters](07-material-characters.md) through [07-5 Materials: Tags and Image Generation](07-material-image.md), and text-to-speech in [11 Text-to-Speech](11-tts.md), [07-3 Materials: Characters](07-material-characters.md), and [07-4 Materials: Voices](07-material-voice.md).
